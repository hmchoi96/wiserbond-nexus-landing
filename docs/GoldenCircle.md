## 0) One-line Definition
**Wiserbond is an offline-first Judgment Memory System that converts decisions into reusable CJR (Condition, Judgment, Core Reasoning) memory, reducing inefficient market noise by preventing corporate amnesia.**

---

## 1) WHY (존재 이유)

### English (Canonical)
Wiserbond exists to reduce inefficient market noise by preserving and reusing an organization’s judgments and core reasoning.  
Modern organizations retain documents, but they lose the “why” behind decisions. When reasoning disappears, teams repeat debates, overreact to headlines, and rebuild judgment from scratch.  
Wiserbond solves corporate amnesia by turning decisions into long-term, reusable memory.

### Korean (Canonical)
Wiserbond는 **조직의 과거 판단과 핵심 추론을 보존하고 재사용**함으로써 **비효율적 시장 노이즈를 줄이기 위해 존재합니다.**  
현대 조직은 문서는 남기지만 “왜 그런 결정을 했는지”를 잃어버립니다.  
그 결과 같은 논쟁을 반복하고, 뉴스에 과잉 반응하며, 판단을 매번 처음부터 다시 쌓게 됩니다.  
Wiserbond는 **Corporate Amnesia**를 해결하는 판단 기억 시스템을 만듭니다.

## 2) HOW (핵심 원리)

Wiserbond is not document search.  
Wiserbond builds a **Judgment Memory System** by converting decision traces into **CJR (Condition, Judgment, Core Reasoning)** data, grounding them with evidence, and connecting them through ontology tags so judgment can be reused over time.

### How Principles (고정 원리 8개)

1) **Upload-first ingestion**
   - Users upload internal decision materials.
   - The system automatically parses and decomposes content into meaningful units.
   - Users should not tune retrieval parameters manually.

2) **CJR candidate extraction with evidence spans**
   - The system proposes CJR candidates.
   - Every candidate must include traceable evidence spans.

3) **Human-in-the-loop confirmation**
   - AI outputs are candidates, not truth.
   - Humans approve or edit quickly.
   - Confirmed CJR becomes training-grade memory.

4) **Ontology tags connect judgment (not just classify)**
   - Tags are connection rules.
   - Shared tags automatically consolidate judgment into clusters.
   - Clusters represent reusable judgment patterns.

5) **CJR-first retrieval, document-second**
   - Retrieval defaults to CJR nodes.
   - Raw documents open only when evidence is required.
   - The product goal is reduced noise, not increased reading.

6) **Reasoning Budget Control**
   - Not every question deserves deep reasoning.
   - Fast mode and deep mode are explicitly separated.
   - Organizations control speed, accuracy, and cost.

7) **Long-context efficiency**
   - The system responds quickly using compressed representations first.
   - It expands to raw evidence only when needed.

8) **Memory management (retention, decay, updates)**
   - Storing everything creates new noise.
   - High-reuse judgments strengthen.
   - Duplicates or invalidated judgments decay or change status.

## 3) WHAT (우리가 제공하는 것)

### English (Canonical)
Wiserbond delivers an installable, offline-first Judgment Memory System.  
It structures Decision and Reasoning memory as CJR, connects it as a reasoning graph, and enables fast, evidence-grounded reuse of judgment.

### Korean (Canonical)
Wiserbond는 **설치형, 오프라인 중심의 Judgment Memory System**입니다.  
조직의 의사결정과 핵심 추론을 **CJR 데이터로 구조화**하고, 태그 기반 그래프로 결집해,  
필요할 때 즉시 꺼내 쓸 수 있는 **재사용 가능한 ‘조직의 판단 뇌’**를 제공합니다.

### The 3 Problems We Solve (고정)

1) **Reasoning disappears**
   - “왜 그렇게 결정했는지”가 문서 속에 묻혀 사라진다.

2) **Judgment does not compound**
   - 같은 조건이 반복돼도 조직이 과거 판단을 재사용하지 못한다.

3) **Evidence is not connected**
   - 근거가 흩어져 “이 판단의 증거가 뭐였지?”가 즉시 나오지 않는다.

> Conclusion:  
> Wiserbond is not enterprise knowledge management (KM).  
> Wiserbond is Decision and Reasoning Memory infrastructure.

## 4) WHO (누구를 위한 것인가)

### Primary customer (고정)
Teams that repeatedly execute macro-driven decisions under uncertainty.

### First target segment (우선순위 1)
**Investment decision teams: PM + Research + Trading**
- Market noise converts directly into cost.
- Macro topics repeat (rates, inflation, tariffs), but judgment is rarely reused.
- Fast feedback loops exist (decision speed, stability, hit rate, drawdown control).

### Best pilot entry point
- Multi-asset / macro teams (high topic repetition)
- PM teams close to risk functions (audit and traceability demand)
- Event-driven macro desks (scenario frequency high)

---

## 5) Boundaries (This is what we are NOT)
- Wiserbond is NOT a general enterprise search engine.
- Wiserbond is NOT a document summarizer without evidence.
- Wiserbond is NOT a fully autonomous decision maker.
- Wiserbond is NOT “more information”.
- Wiserbond IS “reusable judgment with traceable reasoning”.
